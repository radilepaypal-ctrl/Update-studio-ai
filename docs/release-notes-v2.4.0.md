# Timeline Visualizer 2.4.0

Create trip and recap videos from Timeline with local trip detection, saved
projects, presets, advanced video settings, and Library thumbnails. Recap titles
can include your name, and Raw videos use the available recent range with a fixed
100 m accuracy filter.
